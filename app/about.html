About

Nine people, one last, a lot of rain.

Aurelia started in 2019 in a converted bicycle workshop on the Oudegracht. We make performance footwear that can be repaired, and we make it in Portugal.

Designed in Utrecht

Every last, pattern and outsole is developed in-house. No white-label factory catalogues.

Made in Guimarães

One family-run factory, on a four-year contract with published wage floors.

Repaired in Venlo

Our own resole workshop, which has now returned over 14,000 pairs to service.
