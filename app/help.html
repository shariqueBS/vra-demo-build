How long do I have to return a pair?

Sixty days from delivery, worn or unworn. Use the label in your order confirmation email — there is nothing to print.

Can I exchange for a different size?

Yes, once per full-price pair at no cost. Start it from Order history and we reserve the replacement size while your pair is in transit.

What does a resole cost?

€35 including return shipping, or free twice a year on Pro membership. Turnaround is eleven working days.

Do you ship outside the EU?

To the UK, Switzerland and Norway. Duties are shown at checkout and never charged on delivery.

My pair failed resole inspection. What now?

We return them free of charge with a written note on what failed, plus a 20% credit toward a replacement.
